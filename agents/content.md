## Content law
Number or claim in frame one. 45-55 seconds, hard cap 0:55. Re-hook every 8-10s. Dead stop, no outro. Captions short and human. Hook opens a loop (under 5s, visual + audio), substance is 75-80% and edit-driven, payoff closes the loop and ends immediately. Idea test before scripting: two of - relatable to a big audience, raises questions, provokes reaction, seems unrealistic. Format commitment: 10-20 attempts before judging a format. Quality over quantity. Anchor to familiar things. Bright window light, clean lens, arm's-length back camera, eyes on the top third. Same cut IG and TikTok. Weekly two-person whiteboard slot. A visual engine in every video. Caption is the second hook; 3-5 hashtags; location tag.

## Metrics that matter (ignore likes)
- Instagram reels: reach, % non-followers, average watch time and retention curve, 3-second hold, shares, saves, profile visits, follows from post, skip rate. Saves and shares are the north star.
- TikTok: completion rate, average watch time, % from For You, shares, profile views, follows.
- YouTube: impressions, click-through rate (thumbnail + title), average view duration, retention curve (first 30s and the dips), traffic source (search / browse / suggested), subscribers from video, comments.
- Business: leads with source = socials this month, viewings from them, deals from them. The only scoreboard that pays.

## YouTube SEO (before filming, every long-form)
Search intent first: what does the UK buyer type? Title under 60 characters, keyword early. Description: first two lines carry the keyword and the promise, then chapters with timestamps, links, a keyword-rich paragraph. Tags: primary, variants, competitor-adjacent. Thumbnail: face + three words + one number, tested against the last three. Chapters named for search. Pinned comment with the next step. End screen to the one video that converts. Playlists by buyer journey (starter guide, off-plan, Abu Dhabi, deals). Fixed cadence; first-hour engagement matters.
