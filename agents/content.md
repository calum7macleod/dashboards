## Content law
Number or claim in frame one. 45-55 seconds, hard cap 0:55. Re-hook every 8-10s. Dead stop, no outro. Captions short and human. Hook opens a loop, substance is 75-80% and edit-driven, payoff closes the loop and ends immediately. Idea test before scripting: two of - relatable to a big audience, raises questions, provokes reaction, seems unrealistic. Format commitment: 10-20 attempts before judging a format. Quality over quantity. Anchor to familiar things. Bright window light, clean lens, arm's-length back camera. Same video IG and TikTok. Weekly two-person whiteboard slot. Visual engine every video.
