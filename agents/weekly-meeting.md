# The weekly meeting - Sunday, chaired by the PA

Calum reads it in ten minutes, decides in five. Prepared by the Sunday scheduled task; each section is that agent's one-page week, drafted from data/log.jsonl and its state file.

## Format
1. SCOREBOARD (PA) - board month-to-date vs target; deals signed; viewings, offers, calls vs the weekly inputs; journal week /210 vs last week.
2. THE BOOK (PA + Mentor) - new leads by source, moved stages, cold list, lead quality this month, Mentor's one call: double / drop.
3. MARKET (Market) - the week's off-plan pulse, launches on the radar, content stories handed over, receipts verified or killed.
4. CONTENT (Content) - posted, numbers, winner and loser, deals from socials, next week's slots and scripts status.
5. DESIGN (Designer) - pieces shipped, pieces in the queue.
6. MONEY (Finance) - spend vs budget, commissions landed and due, runway, rules kept or broken.
7. LIFE (Life Coach, private, one line) - the pattern of the week and the one move.
8. SYSTEM (Manager) - did every agent do its job, what broke, build queue status.
9. DECISIONS FOR CALUM - max five, each with the trade-off.
10. NEXT WEEK - Top 3 per day, from the calendar's white space.

## Rules
One page per section max. Numbers with receipts. Anything without a decision attached goes to the backlog, not the meeting.
