The Ladder · Amer's 10-Year Version

Compressing 20 Years Into 10

Same engine, honest assumptions - the video runs at 100% ROE per cycle; the plan runs at 50% ROE

◆ The Levers

1. More cash at the start - the single biggest compressor: every rung you skip is 3-4 years saved.
2. Lower payment plans (40/60) - fund 40% instead of 50% and the same market growth returns meaningfully more on your cash. Leverage does the work, not optimism. Timing note: 40/60s are around RIGHT NOW because Abu Dhabi is pushing hard for market share and Dubai developers are competing back - plans this favourable might not always be on the table.
3. Faster cycles (~3-3.5 years) - launch-to-assignment flips rather than always holding to handover.
4. Plan at 50% ROE per cycle, never more - anything the market gives above 50% on your cash is upside, not plan.
5. Bigger projects as cash grows - the ladder doesn't stay sixteen Đ1M units; it becomes fewer, larger positions with the same discipline.
Three ways to run it, next page - capital-led, build-as-you-go, and patient. All three run at 50% ROE per cycle; they differ only in what you start with and what you add. Your calculator has every box editable - break it yourself, then pick your version.
The honesty rail: pushing cycles shorter than ~3 years, or planning above 50% ROE per cycle, makes the plan measurably less likely to hold. The ladder's power is repeatability, not heroics - a plan you can survive a dud rung inside beats a faster plan you can't.

The key mechanic: the £100K a year is never parked waiting for the next rung - it lands straight into LIVE payment plans as it arrives. Each year's top-up either covers the next instalments on units you already hold (freeing ladder cash to open another unit) or opens a fresh unit mid-cycle itself. Every pound is at work from the month it exists - that is what maximising cash-at-work means in practice.
