◆ Why Abu Dhabi for the Flips

Entry, day oneCost
Hudayriyat-type: 5% down + 2% ADM~7%
Dubai typical: 10% down + 4% DLDup to ~14%
Half the entry cost and 2% cheaper fees per cycle. When you are recycling the same pot three times, that 2% saved per turn is real money. 40/60-style plans are around right now because Abu Dhabi is pushing hard for market share - they will not always be this favourable.

◆ The Flip Rules

1. Front-loaded. Flips run 2026-2033, heaviest early. Every dirham in 2026 rides two to three cycles; a 2033 dirham rides none.
2. Never more than half the book. The keepers are the plan; flips are the accelerant.
3. Hard stop 2032-33. No new flips inside the final three years - we are never forced sellers near the finish line.
4. Only flip what we would happily hold. If a flip will not sell, it becomes a rental, not a fire sale. Every pick has to pass that test first.

◆ Purchase 3 - End of 2026, Đ4M

Shortlist: Hudayriyat · Jebel Ali Springs · Taraf on ADGM
Criteria: softest pre-handover plan (40/60-style), handover 2030 or earlier, short-let friendly.

The trade-off, said plainly: flips need a buyer at the right moment. That is why the rules above exist, why Athlon is a gate not a promise, and why the keepers - not the flips - are what actually hit your target.
