Chapter One

What Off-Plan Actually Is

Off-plan means buying directly from the developer before, or while, the property is built. You choose a specific unit from the plan, sign a purchase agreement, and pay in instalments tied to construction - keys usually two to four years later. Here's the whole life of a project, and where you enter it:

Why anyone buys a building that doesn't exist

  • Price: launches are priced below expected completed value - it funds construction and pays you for waiting. That gap is the core opportunity.
  • Instalments, not a mortgage: 10-20% at booking, the rest spread across the build. Many buyers own outright without ever borrowing.
  • First choice of stock: early buyers take the corner plots, park views and high floors - units that carry a premium forever.
  • Currency, for UK buyers: the dirham is pegged to the US dollar - your asset sits in a dollar-linked currency.

One line: off-plan trades time for price. You commit early and wait; in exchange you enter below the finished market with payments spread out. Everything else is detail - important detail, which is what the next twelve pages are.

Chapter Three · Continued

Off-Plan vs Secondary - The Cash Truth

The most under-explained advantage: how much less cash off-plan needs on day one. Buying a completed (secondary) property means a mortgage deposit of at least 20% - fine, that's equity - PLUS ~7% of dead fees in Dubai (4% registration + 2% agency + misc) or ~5% in Abu Dhabi (2% + 2% + misc). Off-plan flips the maths: booking payments start as low as 5-10%, no agency fee, and the registration fee is the only addition.

Day-one cash on a Đ3M (~£640K) purchase

Read the gold bars: a 5% launch plan in Dubai opens the same Đ3M asset for roughly a third of the cash a secondary purchase demands on day one - Đ275K vs Đ810K. That difference is capital you still hold: for the next instalment, the next purchase, or your life. It's also why off-plan returns on CASH INVESTED can be multiples of the headline price growth - Chapter Seven shows that engine.

The case the numbers make

Dubai ran first and hard - roughly doubling from the 2020 trough, now digesting that run with growth flattened to single digits. Abu Dhabi started later and is still accelerating: +17.8% in a year, apartments +24%, and the capital's leading researchers on record that it sits earlier in its property cycle than Dubai. When 84% of a market's sales are off-plan, its buyers have voted where the value is. On today's numbers, for pure capital growth, the capital is the stronger entry.

Why Dubai still wins for many buyers

Deeper market, more liquidity when you exit, the global brand, the bigger rental pool - and the place most buyers actually want to LIVE or holiday. If the purchase is partly a life decision, Dubai's ecosystem is unmatched. Growth rotates; liquidity is structural.

My framing: pure investor, 3-7 year horizon - weight toward Abu Dhabi. A base you'll use, or a first purchase with an eye on living here - Dubai, and buy the community, not the brochure. Many of my buyers now do one of each.

Chapter Seven

The Exits - Four Ways Out (Or Onward)

A purchase without an exit plan is a hope. Off-plan gives you four, and the timing changes the maths dramatically:

1
Sell before handover. Once typically 30-40% is paid (developer NOC required, small fee), you can sell the contract on. Because you've only deployed part of the price, price growth is amplified on your cash - this is where the "doubled their money" stories genuinely come from. If prices rise 20% while you've paid in 30%, the return on your cash is ~60%+ before costs.
2
Sell at handover. The classic maximum-ROE exit: you sell as keys arrive, never paying the final instalment (often the largest, 40-60%). Full price growth, captured on the partial capital you actually deployed.
3
Keep and rent. Take keys, let it - long-term or short-let. UAE gross yields have historically run well above UK city norms, untaxed on the UAE side.
4
Keep, mortgage, extract. At handover, banks lend against COMPLETED value. If the property has grown through the build, a mortgage can release much of your original cash - you keep the asset, redeploy the capital. This is how one purchase becomes a ladder.

A real one, from the public record

A villa in DAMAC Lagoons, Dubai: bought at launch in May 2022 for Đ4,736,000, resold in March 2026 for Đ7,250,000 - +53% on price in under four years (Dubai Land Department records). And because the buyer was on a 50/50 plan, for most of that period only half his money was in the property - the return on cash actually deployed ran far ahead of the headline. That's the off-plan engine: staged capital, full-price growth.

Illustrations of mechanics, not promises - prices move both ways, costs apply to every exit, and Chapter Eight is next for a reason.

Chapter Nine

Cycles - The Only Chart That Matters

UAE property is cyclical and always has been. Pretending otherwise is how people get hurt; reading the cycle is how people get wealthy. The last two decades, in one picture:

What the shape teaches

  • Entry point beats almost everything. Today's "geniuses" mostly just bought when others hesitated.
  • The two cities rotate - Dubai leads, the capital follows with a lag. That's why Chapter Five's answer changes over time, and why serious UAE investors end up owning across both.
  • Hold horizons beat timing. Nobody rings a bell at the bottom; 5-10 year horizons have historically forgiven imperfect timing.
  • Off-plan is a cycle instrument: you commit at today's sentiment and receive at tomorrow's. Powerful entering hesitation; dangerous entering euphoria.
