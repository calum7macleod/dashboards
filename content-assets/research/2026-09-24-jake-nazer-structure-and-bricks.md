## 2. The structure (from full transcripts)
Opening - three slots
1. Title line: flat, searchable. Tells the algorithm the topic.
2. Thesis line, one of: fact + so-what ("only sold freehold since 2019, so you're buying a market seven years old") | conditional verdict ("if you want less risk... Abu Dhabi is the smarter move") | claim ("no developer has delivered more") | promise ("valuable today, valuable forever, here's why").
3. Optional: PDF tease ("I'll tell you how to get it at the end"), then skin ("I own four off-plan villas, over 40m, none in Dubai"). Skin is EARLY in sell videos.

Body - two engines
- Numbered list (locations 1-8, why 1-7, beachfront 1-4). Each item = identity, numbers, distance to ADGM, what's coming, verdict phrase (premium forever / can't be replicated / prime forever).
- Stat cascade (villas, Wadeem, monthly update) = supply block, demand block, one DERIVED ratio, macro backing.
- Rule: every stat carries its so-what in the same breath. "4m barrels a day - which pays for the roads, tunnels, museums, rail before the community is built."
- Rule: he manufactures stats. Two public numbers, one division: one villa per four new ADGM employees; 10.3 Dubai apartments per Abu Dhabi one; one off-plan per 1.6 new residents (Dubai) vs one per 30 (Abu Dhabi).
- Rule: opinion is labelled ("I prefer", "for me", "that's a feeling, not a fact", "my favourite"). Data and opinion never blur.
- Rule: caution inside sell content ("nothing final until the developer confirms", "not confirmed this year despite brokers' rumours", "comment, but random agents will message you").
- Cadence: 5-7 words a point. Rail and distance-to-ADGM inside most location entries.

Close - one of three
- Statement restating the thesis. | Challenge ("if you can't find something this half, you're just not that serious"). | Keyword CTA with a document behind it: guide, master(Marsa), returns, why, brochure, PDF, off-plan updates. Automated delivery likely.
