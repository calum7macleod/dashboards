## 1. Blueprint - one page (Calum, agreed)
Content types
- Evergreen - informative; awareness + consideration combined. Library.
- Time-sensitive - conversion; launches only, still informative. Only a launch that is open now.
Video structure
- Hook: What is X / Why is Y / Explaining Z - searchable, clear on the grid.
- Rehook: a number, statement or claim; opens the loop.
- Deliverable (about 25% of videos): "how to get the analysis at the end".
- 10-30 bricks of dense information, reusable. One relative brick (real-world comparison). One opinion brick, labelled.
- CTA: mainly a statement; occasionally an offer (keyword DM when a document exists).
- Every script converts to a carousel for a second post.
