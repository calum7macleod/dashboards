# Why Dubai Offplan Is Riskier Than Abu Dhabi - reel script (Calum, 19 Oct 2026, v2)

Text: Why Dubai Offplan Is Riskier Than Abu Dhabi
Speech: The Abu Dhabi market is 17 years younger and learned from Dubai's mistakes

Here's the 4 things that lower the risk - the last is most important

One. Rulebook
- Foreigners have only been able to buy property in Abu Dhabi since 2019, in Dubai it's 2002
- Dubai's market fell twice in that time, Abu Dhabi wrote the rules with that in mind
- This impacts the next one more than you would think

Two. Supply vs Demand
- Both cities have the same population size, around 4 million residents
- Abu Dhabi added 288 thousand people and sold 15.9 thousand off-plan properties - 18 new residents per home
- Dubai added 208 thousand people and sold 146 thousand off-plan properties - 1.4 new residents per home
- Abu Dhabi has thirteen times more new residents for every off-plan property sold
- Abu Dhabi has the demand, but the next bit is even more impressive

Three. Finances
- Abu Dhabi has a 1.7 trillion dollar sovereign wealth fund, they could buy every bitcoin on earth
- The Investment Corporation of Dubai is the closest comparison, it has 80 billion dollars net wealth, 20 times smaller
- Abu Dhabi's biggest developers Modon and Aldar's biggest shareholders are these funds, they have the balance sheet to finish what it starts
- The Abu Dhabi government stepped in to financially help Dubai in 2009 with twenty billion dollars

Four. Construction milestones
- I bought offplan in Abu Dhabi this year, my payments are linked to construction milestones
- An independent inspector ensures they are met, if they're not my instalment is delayed
- In Dubai I pay on whatever date the payment plan says even if it is behind

This year many investors are finding Abu Dhabi more attractive than Dubai, this is why
