# Why Dubai Offplan Is Riskier Than Abu Dhabi - reel script (Calum, 19 Oct 2026, v3)

Text: Why Dubai Offplan Is Riskier Than Abu Dhabi
Speech: The Abu Dhabi market is 17 years younger and learned from Dubai's mistakes

4 things lower the risk - the last is most important

One. Rulebook
- Foreigners could only buy property in Abu Dhabi since 2019, in Dubai it's 2002
- Dubai's market fell twice in that time, Abu Dhabi wrote its rules with that in mind
- This impacts the next one more than you would think

Two. Supply vs Demand
- Both cities are around 4 million people
- Abu Dhabi added 288 thousand people and sold 15.9 thousand off-plan properties - 18 new residents per home
- Dubai added 208 thousand people and sold 146 thousand off-plan properties - 1.4 new residents per home
- Thirteen times more new residents for every offplan property sold
- Abu Dhabi has the demand, but the next bit is even more impressive

Three. Finances
- Abu Dhabi has a 1.7 trillion dollar sovereign wealth fund, they could buy every bitcoin on earth
- Dubai's closest equivalent, the Investment Corporation of Dubai, has 80 billion dollars net wealth - 20 times smaller
- Those funds are the biggest shareholders in Modon and Aldar - they've got the balance sheet to finish what they start
- Abu Dhabi stepped in to financially help Dubai in 2009 with twenty billion dollars

Four. Construction milestones
- I bought offplan in Abu Dhabi this year, my payments are linked to construction milestones
- An independent inspector signs each one off. Build's late, my payment's late
- In Dubai I pay on whatever date the payment plan says even if it is behind

More investors are choosing Abu Dhabi over Dubai this year, this is why
