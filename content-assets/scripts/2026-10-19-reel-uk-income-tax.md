# Why Britain Needs Your Income Tax And The UAE Doesn't - reel script (Calum, 19 Oct 2026, v3)

Text: Why Britain Needs Your Income Tax And The UAE Doesn't
Speech: The UK takes 90% of its income from its people's pockets. The UAE takes less than 20% and nothing from your salary

Three things - where it comes from, why the UK can't do without yours, and what it means for your salary. The last one's the bit that matters

First, where it comes from
- The UK takes in 1.6 trillion dollars a year, 40% of its economy
- Income tax 430 billion, National Insurance 260 billion, VAT 280 billion
- The UAE takes in around 155 billion - oil, fees, 5% VAT and a 9% company tax
- Nothing from salaries - and here's why

Second, why the UK needs yours
- The UK spends 44% of its economy and collects 40% - it borrows the difference every single year
- No surplus this century, the interest on the debt is now 150 billion dollars, more than defence
- The UAE collects 28% and spends 23% - a surplus every year, and a 1.7 trillion dollar fund
- The UK needs your income tax because it's already spent it - the UAE doesn't because it saved

Third, what it means for your salary
- A 100 thousand dollar salary in the UK - 24 thousand income tax, 5 thousand in National Insurance, then 20% VAT on what you spend
- The UAE has neither of them, and only 5% VAT
- In the UAE if you get paid 100 thousand dollars you get 100 thousand. In the UK you only get 72 thousand dollars

The UAE has added two taxes in twenty years and still doesn't need yours. Britain added none and takes more than a third of the economy. This is why.
