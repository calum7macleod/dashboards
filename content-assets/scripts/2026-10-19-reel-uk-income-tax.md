# Why Britain Needs Your Income Tax And The UAE Doesn't - reel script (Calum, 19 Oct 2026)

Text: Why Britain Needs Your Income Tax And The UAE Doesn't
Speech: The UK takes 90% of its income from its people's pockets. The UAE takes less than 10% and nothing from your salary

Here's where the money actually comes from, why the UK can't do without yours, and what that means for your salary - the last one's the bit that matters

First, where it comes from
- The UK government takes in 1.6 trillion dollars a year, 40% of everything the economy earns
- Income tax 430 billion, National Insurance 260 billion, VAT 280 billion
- The UAE government takes in around 155 billion dollars - oil, government fees, 5% VAT and a 9% company tax
- None of it from salaries - and here's why that gap exists

Second, why the UK needs yours
- The UK spends 44% of its economy and collects 40% - it borrows the difference every single year
- It hasn't run a surplus this century, the interest on the debt is now 175 billion dollars, more than it spends on defence
- The UAE collects 28% and spends 23% - a surplus every year, backed by a 1.7 trillion dollar fund
- The UK needs your income tax because it has already spent it - the UAE doesn't because it saved

Third, what it means for your salary
- A 100 thousand dollar salary in the UK - around 24 thousand in income tax, 5 thousand in National Insurance, then 20% VAT on what you spend
- The same salary here has neither of them, and only 5% on what you spend
- In the UAE if you get paid 100 thousand dollars you get 100 thousand. In the UK you get 72 thousand
- (optional) In the UK it costs your employer 114 thousand dollars to put 72 in your account. Here, 100 goes in and 100 comes out.

The UAE has added two taxes in twenty years and still doesn't need yours. Britain has added none and takes a third of the economy. This is why.
