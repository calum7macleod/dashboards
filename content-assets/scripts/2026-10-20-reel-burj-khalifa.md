# Why Dubai's Most Famous Building Is Named After Abu Dhabi's Ruler - reel script (Calum, 20 Oct 2026)

Text: Why Dubai's Most Famous Building Is Named After Abu Dhabi's Ruler
Speech: It was called Burj Dubai for 6 years, until the day it opened, here's why

Four key dates - the most important is opening night

6th January 2004
- Construction started under the name Burj Dubai

25th November 2009
- Dubai World - the government-owned company behind the Palm - asked its lenders for six months' grace on 26 billion dollars of debt
- Stock markets around the world fell the next morning. Dubai's own market fell seven per cent

14th December 2009
- Abu Dhabi provided ten billion dollars to Dubai - reported as a loan - to cover a bond due that day
- Abu Dhabi could do that because it had been saving its oil money since 1967
- Today those funds hold 1.7 trillion dollars, including around 0.5% of the entire world's stock market

4th January 2010
- The tallest building in the world opened. Three weeks after the money, Sheikh Mohammed announced its name: the Burj Khalifa, in honour of Sheikh Khalifa - the President of the UAE and the ruler of Abu Dhabi

The loan was extended in 2014 and again in 2019. The name hasn't changed since.
