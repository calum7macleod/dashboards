# How To Buy Off-Plan In Abu Dhabi From The UK - reel script (Calum, 20 Oct 2026, v2 - trimmed)

Text: How To Buy Off-Plan In Abu Dhabi From The UK
Speech: It takes you under an hour, and you don't even need to be here to buy multi-million-dollar properties

Here's what happens, in order, with my advice at the end

First, plan your budget and outcome
- It's not like the UK - launches get announced and a week later it's sold out
- Have a plan and stick to it
- A good broker knows about upcoming projects before they're announced

Second, EOI
- Some projects have such high demand you pay the developer a refundable deposit first, called an expression of interest, to get an invite to the next bit

Third, booking day
- Abu Dhabi demand is so high your broker can queue for hours
- When they're seated, you get units to pick from, on a call with your broker
- Decide your desired locations before the call, so you're choosing, not panicking
- You pay your first instalment at booking - any EOI comes off it

Fourth, contract
- The developer sends you a contract by DocuSign - you sign it and wait for them to counter-sign
- Some send them immediately, some take weeks
- Once signed, it's yours

Plan well and execute - it's the difference between a property you love and one you regret

I bought off-plan here myself this year. I love mine
