# Why I Will Leave Dubai For Hudayriyat Island By 2030 - reel script (Calum, 20 Oct 2026)

Text: Why I Will Leave Dubai For Hudayriyat Island By 2030
Speech: I've lived in Dubai for 6 years, here's why it's time to leave

Five reasons - the last one is the most debatable

One. Hudayriyat amenities
- 2 championship golf courses, 12 luxury hotels, five marinas, 6 malls, 16 international schools and 7 healthcare centres are planned
- Already open is the world's largest surf pool, velodrome, tennis centre, beach clubs and a 5 star hotel - long before villas hand over
- Abu Dhabi plans ahead, Dubai plans behind, as you're about to see

Two. Infrastructure
- Abu Dhabi is slower than Dubai - it started selling property to foreigners in 2019, Dubai was 17 years earlier
- It's building a high speed rail and oversized roads before the population increases
- Hudayriyat has 3 tunnels and 3 bridges confirmed, it will be much easier to get on and off than the Palm in Dubai
- Speaking of the Palm...

Three. Density
- You could fit 9 and a half Palm Jumeirahs on Hudayriyat
- The Palm has 6,250 residents per square kilometre, Hudayriyat will have half - 3,100

Four. Value for money
- I live in a townhouse in the Dubai desert, 30 minutes from the beach, valued at around 390 dollars a square foot
- On Hudayriyat I can get a villa on an island, by the beach, for around 330
- Modon's main shareholder is the government, they are building to blow away, not to profit like Dubai's
- It needs to appreciate 20% to cost the same as my house, I think it will do a lot more than that, and I would pay it for the better quality

Five. Lifestyle
- I am older and I have a baby, Abu Dhabi is slower and that suits me
- It feels more traditional and higher quality
- It aligns more with my way of life

I will move to Hudayriyat because of all this, and I can't wait
