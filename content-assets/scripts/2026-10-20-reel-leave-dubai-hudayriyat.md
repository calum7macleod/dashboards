# Why I Will Leave Dubai For Hudayriyat Island By 2030 - reel script (Calum, 20 Oct 2026, v2 - trimmed)

Text: Why I Will Leave Dubai For Hudayriyat Island By 2030
Speech: I've lived in Dubai for 6 years, here's why it's time to leave

The last one is the most debatable

One. Hudayriyat amenities
- 2 championship golf courses, 12 luxury hotels, 5 marinas, 6 malls, 16 international schools and 7 clinics are planned
- Already open is the world's largest surf pool, a velodrome, a tennis centre, beach clubs and a 5 star resort - before a single villa has handed over
- Abu Dhabi plans ahead, Dubai plans behind, as I'm about to show you

Two. Infrastructure
- Abu Dhabi started selling property to foreigners in 2019, 17 years after Dubai
- High speed rail and oversized roads are going in before the population increases
- Hudayriyat will have 3 tunnels and 3 bridges - much easier to get on and off than the Palm
- Speaking of the Palm...

Three. Density
- You could fit 9 and a half Palm Jumeirahs on Hudayriyat
- The Palm: 6,250 people per square kilometre. Hudayriyat: half that, with 3,100

Four. Villa quality
- My Dubai townhouse - in the desert, 30 minutes from the beach - is worth around 390 dollars a square foot
- A villa on Hudayriyat island, by the beach: around 330
- Modon's main shareholder is the government. I believe they are building to blow you away, not to profit like Dubai's
- It needs 20% appreciation to match my house. I think it'll do a lot more, and I'd pay for the quality

Five. Lifestyle
- I am older and I have a baby. Abu Dhabi is slower and that suits me
- It feels more traditional and higher quality
- It aligns more with my way of life

I will move to Hudayriyat because of all this, and I can't wait
