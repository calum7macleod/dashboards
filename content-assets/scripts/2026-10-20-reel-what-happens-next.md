# I Bought Offplan In Abu Dhabi, What Happens Next - reel script (Calum, 20 Oct 2026, v2 - trimmed)

Text: I Bought Offplan In Abu Dhabi, What Happens Next
Speech: I signed in April. Here's what's happened since, and what's still to come

Five things - the most important is last

First, portal
- You get a login. Construction updates, your statement of account, every payment and its date

Two, registration
- You paid 2% ADM fee to the government and it's logged with ADREC, Abu Dhabi's registry
- You get the interim registration certificate - the title deed for before it's handed over

Three, payments
- Mine are 5% at each construction stage - early works, podium, structure, facade - checked by an independent engineer before each one is due
- Targeted every 6 months, totalling 40%, with 60 due at handover
- The money sits in escrow. The developer draws it down only when milestones are hit
- That's my protection

Four, selling early
- Once 20% is paid, you can sell
- Before that, you or the new buyer would need to pay the developer up to 20% before the transfer

Five, handover
- You get keys and the title deed, and it's yours
- Live in it, sell, rent or short-term let it
- Many owners sell just before handover for optimal return on cash - in my case that's 40%
