# I Bought Offplan In Abu Dhabi, What Happens Next - reel script (Calum, 20 Oct 2026)

Text: I Bought Offplan In Abu Dhabi, What Happens Next
Speech: I signed in April. Here's what's happened since, and what's still to come

Here's the five things - the most important is the last

First, portal
- You get a login. Construction updates, your statement of account, every payment and its date, all in one place

Two, registration
- You paid 2% - that's the fee to the government - and it gets logged with ADREC, Abu Dhabi's real estate registry
- They give you the interim registration certificate, the title deed for before it's handed over

Three, payments
- Mine are 5% at each construction stage - early works, podium, structure, facade - checked by an independent engineer before each one is due
- The target for the developer is every 6 months, totalling 40%, with 60 due at handover
- The money sits in escrow. The developer draws it down only when milestones are hit
- This protects me

Four, selling early
- Once 20% is paid, you can sell
- If you're not that far through the payment plan you can still sell, but you or the new buyer will need to pay the developer up to 20% before the transfer

Five, handover
- When you get the keys and title deed, the property is ready to be lived in
- You can live in it, sell, rent or short-term let it
- Many owners sell just before handover - meaning in my case I'd only have paid 40%, and maximised my return on cash
