## 7. NEW - The Abu Dhabi To Dubai Train Is Running (And Nobody's Talking About It)
Speech: 39 dirhams, 57 minutes, ten trains a day - the Abu Dhabi to Dubai train opened last month. Here's what it changes for property, and what it doesn't
Three things, the last is the honest bit
One. What opened
- September 30th. Etihad Rail's passenger service - Abu Dhabi to Dubai in 57 to 64 minutes, ten trains a day, from 39 dirhams in Comfort, 109 in Premium. 11 dollars for the cheapest seat
- The UAE President rode the first train. 150,000 people had already used the Fujairah line before Dubai opened
- Al Dhafra stations open in December, Sharjah in March. So where does it actually stop?
Two. Where it stops
- Abu Dhabi: Mohammed Bin Zayed City - the mainland, 25 minutes from the islands. Dubai: Al Yalayis, by Jumeirah Golf Estates - 25 minutes from Downtown
- So door to door, Reem to DIFC is still about two hours. The train's the middle hour
- Here's why it matters anyway
Three. What it changes - and what it doesn't
- The objection was never the hour on the motorway. It was that there was no alternative to it. Now there is one, and it's 39 dirhams
- The honest bit: this is the 200 km/h line. The 350 km/h high-speed link - 30 minutes, with stops at ADGM, Saadiyat and Yas - is the one that moves island prices, and it's 2031
- Dubai's Metro opened in 2009 and every station got a price premium within five years. The high-speed stations are announced; the premium hasn't arrived yet
The train's here. The fast one's coming. The stations are on the map - and that's the window
