## 4. How Can The UAE Afford To Run With No Income Tax
Speech: The UAE government takes in around 155 billion dollars a year and almost none of it is from your salary. Here's where it comes from
Four things, the last is the one nobody says
One. Oil
- Over half of it. ADNOC pumps around 4 million barrels a day; its dividends, royalties and taxes go to the Abu Dhabi government
- Britain had North Sea oil too - it spent the money. Abu Dhabi saved it. Here's what that saving does now
Two. The funds
- Around 1.9 trillion dollars across three sovereign funds, built from oil since 1967. The returns alone are tens of billions a year, before a barrel is sold
- Norway's fund is bigger. Norway also has 25% VAT and income tax up to 47%. Abu Dhabi has 5% and none
- And since 2018, two more sources
Three. The new taxes
- 5% VAT since 2018, 9% corporate tax since 2023 - on companies, not people. Together roughly a fifth of the take
- Plus fees: visas, licences, the 2% on your property. All of it on transactions, none on earnings
- So does it add up?
Four. The surplus
- The UAE collects about 28% of its economy and spends 23. A surplus, most years. Britain collects 40 and spends 44, borrowing the gap every year
- The honest bit: it leans on oil, and oil prices move. That's why the funds exist - and why the 9% company tax arrived
- No income tax isn't a loophole. It's a balance sheet with 1.9 trillion on one side
The UAE doesn't need your salary because it saved its oil money. Britain needs yours because it didn't. DM OFFPLAN for info.

## 5. Abu Dhabi Supply Can't Keep Up With Demand
Speech: 18 new residents arrived for every off-plan home sold in Abu Dhabi last year. Unless 18 people share a bedroom, something has to give
Three things, the last is what gives
One. The gap
- The census added 288,000 people to Abu Dhabi in a year. Developers sold 15,900 off-plan homes. 18 to one
- In Dubai it's around 2 to one - 332,000 people, 146,000 homes
- Even if every new household was a family of four, Abu Dhabi needed four times what it sold. Here's the proof it's real
Two. Rents
- Apartment rents up 17% in six months - the regulator's number. Villas up too. Rents only move like that when people can't find a home
- ADGM added 12,400 jobs - a 34% jump - and most of them rent first. That's the demand side
- And the supply side can't speed up
Three. What gives
- Abu Dhabi has around 20,000 homes under construction across the islands - Yas 7,700, Fahid 3,500, Saadiyat 3,200. Against 290,000 new people a year, that's two years of growth with no homes at all
- Launches sell out in days because the people are already here. Golf Estates, Yas Point, Bashayer - gone before the brochure dried
- The catch: Dubai said the same in 2007 and built too much by 2009. Abu Dhabi's answer is the escrow law and milestone payments - developers can't build what they haven't sold. The brake is built in
Supply can't catch up this decade. Prices and rents are how a market says so. DM OFFPLAN for info.
