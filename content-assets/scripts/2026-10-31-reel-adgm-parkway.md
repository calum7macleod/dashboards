Three things, the last is why it matters to you

One. The road
- Named the Mid-Island Parkway - 780 million dollars, eight lanes from Saadiyat's north to Reem and the main Abu Dhabi island, with a 600-metre section under the water
- Phase one finishes in 2027. Saadiyat to Reem: 25 minutes today, 10 when it opens. The full route puts Saadiyat and Zayed City - the two ends of the capital - under 15 minutes apart
- But what's so important about ADGM?

Two. Why ADGM
- 49,000 people work in the financial district, up 34% this year. They live on Reem, or they live on Saadiyat and sit in traffic
- Ten minutes means the museums, the beach and Cranleigh school are a school-run away. That's the whole of London's Zone 1 and Richmond in one 10-minute commute
- And it's part of something bigger

Three. The plan
- 46 billion dollars on roads and transport by 2030: this parkway, the Hudayriyat tunnels, a tram from the airport to Yas, the high-speed rail
- Dubai built its roads after the towers. Abu Dhabi's building them before the people arrive
- The catch: 2027 for phase one, the rest after - so the drive's still 25 minutes for another year

I bought off-plan on Reem in April. Next year the beach is ten minutes from my door
