Five things - the last one's the catch

One. What it is
- Modon's first villa community on Hudayriyat - the island that's had the biggest launch in UAE history since
- Four to six bedrooms, 7,200 to 8,400 square feet, the first homes that will hand over on the island
- And nobody's talking about it

Two. What happened
- It sold out in January. Then came Golf Estates, the hills, Bashayer - and every broker on the island moved on to the next launch
- The conflict in the spring broke the thread between January's buyers and today's
- Ninety-odd villas are listed for resale right now. Here's what they cost

Three. What it costs
- A 4-bed, 7,200 square feet, from around 7.75 million dirhams - 2.1 million dollars. That's around 1,100 dirhams a foot, 290 dollars
- Golf Estates townhouses launched at 1,800 a foot. Nawayef at 2,150. Al Naseem is a little over half the island's price per foot, and it hands over first
- Some are listed at the original launch price - no premium. Which brings me to why

Four. Why it's still the best buy
- It's built out first, beside the surf pool, the velodrome and the beach that are already open
- Under 2.5 million dollars for a standalone villa on an island, twenty minutes from the financial district - that's a two-bed apartment on Saadiyat
- The developer's sold out. The only way in is resale. And resale is the catch

Five. The catch
- To transfer, the seller needs to have paid 20% - so you're paying the balance up to 20%, plus the premium if there is one, in cash. More up front than a new launch
- Handover was Q4 2026. I'd now plan for summer 2028 - eighteen months later
- Villas, not apartments - so it's a lifestyle buy or a patient one
