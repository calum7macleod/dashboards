Four things - the last one's about one of the UAE's biggest developers

One. Who are Apollo
- A trillion dollars under management, Wall Street's biggest private lender. It owns Yahoo, and put 11 billion dollars into Intel's chip factory in Ireland - one of the biggest private financing deals in the history of the planet
- Its boss, Marc Rowan, was on the shortlist to run the US Treasury. When he moves money, people watch where
- And I'm going to tell you his most recent move

Two. The deal
- A little over a billion dollars for just under 10% of Mubadala Capital - the investing arm of Abu Dhabi's 385 billion dollar fund. That values it at over ten billion
- Plus five billion more committed to its funds, with other investors following
- Not a loan. Not a partnership. Ownership
- And this is only the start

Three. Why
- Apollo's boss said recently: "You are going to see us ramp up our activity here tremendously"
- "Is it riskier today? Common sense says it's riskier"
- "But everyone has adapted. We have less unknown than we did"
- Apollo's money hasn't stopped at just the fund

Four. Aldar loan
- In February, Apollo lent Aldar - the developer of Saadiyat, Yas and Fahid - one billion dollars
- Wall Street's biggest lender has now bet on Abu Dhabi's fund and developer in the same year
- Apollo's business is to assess risk and act when the upside is much bigger than the downside. They acted on Abu Dhabi
