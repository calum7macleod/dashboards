# Bvlgari Island reel (31 Oct 2026, v2 - trimmed) - for 5 Nov recording

Text: You've Probably Never Heard Of Abu Dhabi's Most Exclusive Island
Speech: 90 mansions, no prices, no launch - you're invited or you're not

Three things, the last one's the money

One. What it is
- A private island off the Corniche, next to the Emirates Palace, with the Presidential Palace across the water. The best address in Abu Dhabi
- Bulgari - one of the world's most exclusive jewellery brands - expanded into hotels in the world's most prestigious cities: Rome, Milan, Paris, London, Dubai. And now Abu Dhabi
- Mohamed Alabbar is building it - the man who founded EMAAR and built the Burj Khalifa. When he picks a site, it's the one everyone wanted
- But the masterplan is even more impressive

Two. What's on it
- A hotel with just 60 rooms - the Emirates Palace next door has nearly 400 - and 30 beach villas. Small on purpose, to stay private
- One house to rent, not buy: 12,900 square feet, thirteen average UK homes, with a 20-metre pool and its own beach
- 90 mansions to own - up to 27,000 square feet, eight with a private beach, some with a dock for a 25-metre yacht
- Opening 2030. So what does one cost?

Three. The money
- Nobody knows. No price list, no brochure, no booking day. Sold the way Hermes sells a Birkin - you don't apply, you're offered
- The only guide is Bulgari's Dubai island, where a penthouse sold for 112 million dollars - Dubai's record
- This one's bigger, quieter, and looks at a palace

The most expensive address in Abu Dhabi, and nobody's heard of it. Send this to whoever thinks Dubai's the expensive one. DM ISLAND for the PDF.
