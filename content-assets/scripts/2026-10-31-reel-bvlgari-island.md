Text: You've Probably Never Heard Of Abu Dhabi's Most Exclusive Island
Speech: 90 mansions, no prices, no launch - you're invited or you're not

Three things, the last one's the money

One. What it is
- A private island off the Corniche, next to the Emirates Palace - the most famous hotel in the city - with the Presidential Palace in view across the water. The best address Abu Dhabi has, by location alone
- Bulgari - one of the world's most exclusive jewellery brands - expanded into hotels in the world's most prestigious cities: Rome, Milan, Paris, London, Dubai. And now Abu Dhabi
- The developer is Mohamed Alabbar - the man who founded EMAAR and built the Burj Khalifa. When he picks a site, it's the one everyone else wanted
- But the masterplan of the island is even more impressive

Two. What's on it
- A hotel with just 60 rooms and 30 beach villas - kept small so it stays private
- One house to rent, not buy: 12,900 square feet, thirteen average UK homes, with a 20-metre pool and its own beach
- And 90 mansions to own - from 17,800 up to 27,000 square feet, eight with a private beach, some with a dock for a 25-metre yacht at the end of the garden
- Opening 2030. So what does one cost?

Three. The money
- Nobody knows. No price list, no brochure, no booking day - the mansions go by invitation, to people the developer chooses
- The only guide is Bulgari's Dubai island, where one penthouse sold for 112 million dollars - the most ever paid for a home in Dubai
- This one's bigger, quieter, and looks at a palace

The most expensive address in Abu Dhabi, and nobody's heard of it. DM ISLAND for the PDF on all eight.
