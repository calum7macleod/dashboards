# How Can The UAE Afford To Run With No Income Tax - reel (Calum, FINAL 4 Nov 2026 - six pots) - for 5 Nov recording

Hook (Calum, FINAL 4 Nov): How The UAE Runs With No Income Tax | Here's how the country survives with a big hole in its accounts  [Calum's call - body says surplus]

6 pots - I think I contributed 100% of the last one

One. Oil - 45%
- 45% of the UAE's 158 billion dollars annual income comes from oil
- ADNOC pumps around 4 million barrels a day; its dividends, royalties and taxes go to the Abu Dhabi government
- Britain had North Sea oil too - it spent the money. Abu Dhabi saved it. Here's what that saving does now

Two. Sovereign wealth funds - 4%
- 1.9 trillion dollars across three funds, built from oil since 1967. The returns alone are tens of billions a year, before a barrel is sold  [dirhams - see note]
- Norway's fund is bigger. Norway also has 25% VAT and income tax up to 47%. Abu Dhabi has 5% and none

Three. The new taxes - 18%
- 5% VAT since 2018, 9% corporate tax since 2023 - on companies rather than people
- On a 100 thousand dollar salary, the UK takes 28 thousand before you spend a penny. Here it takes nothing

Four. Fees - 20%
- Property transfers, trade licences, visas, utilities and tolls
- Dubai's 4% DLD and Abu Dhabi's 2% registration fee are a property tax on all transactions

Five. Everything else - 12%
- The country has a lot of money - it makes interest and sells land to developers
- Emirati locals make pension contributions, unlike residents

Six. Fines - 1%
- I make up a lot of this in speeding fines. I've got better, but they are ruthless
- 1.6 billion dollars a year - more than the UK collects in speeding fines, from a country six times smaller  [Calum had 'roughly the same... 15 times smaller' - UK take is a few hundred million GBP; UAE is ~6x smaller by population. v Market]

The surplus
- The UAE collects about 28% of its economy and spends 23. A surplus, most years. Britain collects 40 and spends 44, borrowing the difference every year
- Only around 30 countries in the world are in a surplus, and that number drops every year
- The honest bit: it leans on oil, and oil prices move. That's why the funds exist - and why the 9% company tax arrived
- No income tax is thanks to a 1.9 trillion dollar fund and oil, and will continue for many years to come

The UAE doesn't need your salary because it saved its oil money. Britain needs yours because it didn't
