Speech: Four million barrels of oil a day. OpenAI decides where they come from and who buys them

- Here's the deal, and how it impacts property prices
- One. The deal
  - ADNOC, one of the world's biggest oil companies, signed a multi-year deal with OpenAI and Abu Dhabi's own AI company, G42
  - MGX, part of the city's 1.9 trillion dollar sovereign wealth funds, has invested 1.5 billion dollars in OpenAI between 2024 and today - now worth 6 billion  [FLAG: MGX = Mubadala + G42 vehicle, 'backed by' safer than 'part of'; ~$5.9bn is a cap-table estimate - 'around 6']

  - Sam Altman flew in to sign it
  - Oil companies have used AI for years. What's new is the scale, and that it's OpenAI
  - So why should a property investor care?
- Two. The money
  - At today's price ADNOC pumps around 400 million dollars of oil a day
  - Make that just 1% more efficient and it's 1.5 billion dollars a year  [illustration: 1% of ~$146bn/yr at $100]
  - Oil is 45% of the UAE's income - more profit means more money to invest in making the city better
  - Better city, higher property prices - and that's only half of it
- Three. The jobs
  - OpenAI's first Stargate outside America is being built in Abu Dhabi - part of a 5-gigawatt AI campus, switching on this year
  - Microsoft has put 1.5 billion dollars into G42
  - When the hedge funds came to ADGM, its workforce grew 34% in a year and island rents rose 21%
  - The AI engineers are next, and they need somewhere to live
- The oil pays for the AI. The AI makes the oil pay more. The city gets better with more people, and property prices increase
