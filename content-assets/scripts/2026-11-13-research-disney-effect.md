# Disney effect on property - research for reel (13 Nov 2026)

- ANAHEIM (1955): Disney bought 160 acres of orange groves for $879,000 (~$5,500/acre). Population 14,556 (1950) -> 104,000+ (1960). 25+ hotels/motels around the park by 1959. Walt said he regretted the land speculation he couldn't control. (anaheim.gov; OC History; ClickOrlando)
  Caveat: post-war Southern California boom also drove growth.
- ORLANDO (1965 buy, 1971 open): 27,443 acres (2x Manhattan) for ~$5m, under $200/acre. After the announcement a nearby acre sold for $131,000 (thewaltdisneycompany.com). By 1971 land going for $75,000-150,000/acre, up to 500x 1965 value (BusinessWeek 11 Sep 1971). Region <0.5m people mid-60s -> Orlando metro ~2.7m today.
- PARIS (1992): Chessy a farming village; Val d'Europe grew to 54,000+ residents and 9,300 businesses (SETEC study, 2026). Up to EUR 120bn value added to French economy since 1992; 445m visits (DLP fact sheet FY26).
- SHANGHAI (approved Nov 2009, opened 2016): 1.5 hours after approval a nearby plot sold at 264% of its reserve (China Daily 4 Nov 2009). Chuansha home prices +162% 2009-2015 vs Shanghai +101% (SCMP 2016). Savills: short-term frenzy on announcement, then back to city-average growth.
- HONG KONG (2005) - the catch: smallest Disney, isolated on Lantau; losses in 8 of first 11 years (Attractions Mgmt/SCMP); no comparable property story found.
- YAS ISLAND: Disneyland Abu Dhabi announced May 2025 (Miral). Yas apartments +24% in 2025, +18% to June 2026 [v - restamp source before filming].
- PATTERN: the jump comes at the announcement and during construction, not at opening (Orlando 1965 vs 1971; Shanghai 2009 vs 2016). Yas is in that window now.
