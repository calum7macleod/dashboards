# Why I Quit Dubai Real Estate - reel script (Calum, 16 Nov 2026)

Text: Why I Quit Dubai Real Estate
Speech: Five dark truths that made me leave

Most agents would try to hide the last one

One. Other agents
- A lot are unregistered, sometimes registered ones are worse
- The barrier to entry is so low, a high percentage of agents are incompetent
- They often struggle financially which can make them do corrupt things
- The next group of people can be worse

Two. Owners
- Everyone thinks they own the best unit in the development
- They play agents and buyers against each other
- Speaking of which

Three. People looking to buy
- They could go for 3 viewings over 2 weeks with you then offer on the same unit with another agent
- That exact thing once happened to me

Four. Emotion
- End users want to buy somewhere they love to live in
- I'm a numbers guy
- Which is why the next one's important

Five. Commission
- Offplan can pay agents up to 4 times as much
- I will always advise clients based on their best interest, I also value my time so would rather work with people who want that from the start
- It's a win win, on a ready sale the client pays 2% commission, here the developer pays it

I will still be in the industry but spending my time on the offplan market rather than ready sales
