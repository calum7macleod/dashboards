# 8 Things UK Property Investors Hate and What the UAE Does Instead

The UK taxes the rent, sale and death. The UAE has one fee here's everything else that's better and worse

- The UK is better in the ones at the end
- One tax on rent
  - The UK takes 20, 40 or 45%, and it goes up 2 more next April
  - The UAE takes nothing
- Two stamp duty
  - A second property in the UK costs an extra 5%, and 2% more if you live abroad
  - Abu Dhabi charges 2% once, Dubai 4%, the only government cost for transactions
- Three tax when you sell
  - The UK takes up to 24% of your profit
  - The UAE takes none
- Four tax when you die
  - The UK takes 40% above the threshold
  - The UAE has no inheritance tax, but register a will here or local law decides who gets it
- Five mortgage interest
  - The UK taxes your rent before your mortgage is paid
  - With no tax on rent, that problem doesn't exist here
- Six residency
  - A UK property gets you no right to live there
  - A 410 thousand pound property here gets you a 10 year visa
  - The next two are where the UK wins
- Seven borrowing
  - A UK landlord can borrow 75%
  - Living abroad, expect about 60% here, at a higher rate
- Eight rent rises
  - Dubai caps them with a government index
  - You can't always charge what the market would pay
- Six better, two worse, and now you know which is which
