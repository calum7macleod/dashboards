# Abu Dhabi Has A Property Supply Crisis

Draft v1 - 19 Nov 2026 - c86 - built on what worked in "Who Is Actually Going To Live" (c36)
~145 words, ~55 seconds. Setting: board (two numbers on screen: 72,000 needed / 9,000 being built).

## Hook

**Text:** Abu Dhabi Has A Property Supply Crisis

**Speech:** Everyone asks if Abu Dhabi is building too much. It needs 8 times more than it's building.

## Script

Here's the maths.

Abu Dhabi added 288 thousand people last year.

At four people a home, that's 72 thousand homes. For one year.

There are 37 thousand being built. For the next four years.

That's 9 thousand a year, against 72 thousand needed. 8 times short.

Now look at Dubai.

It's growing at about the same pace, and it has 322 thousand homes under construction. Nearly nine times more.

"But not everyone who arrives rents a place on an island." True. So cut the number in half. It's still four times short.

And the jobs haven't slowed. The financial centre grew its workforce 34 percent in a year.

So when prices rise 18 percent in twelve months, that isn't a bubble.

It's a queue.

## Caption

Abu Dhabi has a property supply crisis - and it's not the one people think.

288,000 people arrived last year. At four to a home that's 72,000 homes. There are 37,000 under construction - for the next four years.

Dubai is growing at a similar pace with 322,000 on the way.

Halve the demand number and it's still four times short.

Send this to the person who told you it's overbuilt.

## Flags - Market to stamp before filming

- 288,000 = people added to Abu Dhabi emirate last year (previous year, not conflict period).
- 37,000 (36,900) = homes under construction 2026-30, Knight Frank Jul 2026. It is "being built", NOT a handover schedule - script says "being built", never "handing over".
- 322,000 = Dubai under active construction by 2030, ValuStrat Aug 2026. Dubai +332k people vs Abu Dhabi +288k, ~8% each - "about the same pace".
- "Four people a home" is an assumption - say it as one. 72,000 / 9,250 = 7.8, rounded to 8.
- 34% = ADGM workforce growth to 49,000.
- 18% = apartment price rise, year to June 2026 (from the YouTube islands script) - confirm scope (Reem / city-wide) or cut the line and end on "four times short... that isn't oversupply. It's a queue."
- The halving line is deliberate: the top comment will be "most arrivals are workers in shared accommodation". Say it first.
