## Hook

**Text:** Abu Dhabi has a Property Supply Crisis

**Speech:** The city needs 8 times more properties than it's handing over in the next 4 years

## Script (v3 - tightened 19 Nov, ~107 words, ~42 seconds)

Abu Dhabi has 37 thousand properties due by 2030.

At 4 people each, that's room for 148 thousand.

It added 288 thousand people last year alone. Nearly double.

At that pace, over a million more by 2030.

They need 288 thousand properties. It's building 37. 8 times short.

Dubai, growing at the same pace, is building 322 thousand.

And new projects take 4 years. Anything launched today lands after 2030.

Limited supply, rising demand, prices go up.

That's why I invested in Abu Dhabi myself.
