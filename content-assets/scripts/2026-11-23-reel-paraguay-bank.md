## Facts
- Ueno: ~3m customers; deposits $2.8-2.9bn (~9% of system); $146m to owner-linked companies (founder Miguel Vazquez's group); public funds ~$800-850m; pension share $355m-$516m by source; guarantee ~$37k covers ~97% of customers; ~1/3 of deposits not expected back (CSIS 20 Nov).
- President Santiago Pena: former shareholder in Ueno's holding company (sold 2025); appointed central bank chief Carlos Carvallo (ex-Ueno predecessor); accused of steering public funds in. Resigned 5 Nov, removed by Senate 44-0, house arrest 6 Nov pending 7 Nov hearing; charges breach of trust, influence peddling, use of office for gain, up to 10 years. Denies. Status after 7 Nov NOT confirmed. The $146m loans were NOT to him. VP Pedro Alliana now president.
- UAE banks (June 2026): FAB AED 1.41tn (Mubadala 37.9% + ruling family 17.7%), Emirates NBD 1.32tn (Dubai govt ~56%), ADCB 833bn (Mubadala 60.8%), DIB 423bn, Mashreq 366bn (private, ~7%). System AED 5.8tn assets, 3.57tn deposits (Sep 2026). Top three ~64% - indicative, includes overseas books. "80% government-linked" is Content's estimate, unstamped.
