# YouTube channel - execution plan
Proposed by Content to Calum, 24 Nov 2026. Status: PROPOSED - awaiting Calum's go on the promise, the release day and the targets.

## Principle
Title and thumbnail first (80% of the result). Then the first 30 seconds. Then one ask per video. Nothing is filmed until its title and thumbnail are agreed.

## Promise (to confirm)
Abu Dhabi property for UK investors, from someone who put his own money in.

## Phase 0 - Foundations, 24-30 Nov (no filming)
1. Evidence: Calum sends 3 YouTube Studio screenshots (channel page, Reach tab and retention graph for the Hudayriyat tour). Content: search research (what UK investors type), teardown of 5 competing channels, topic bank of 30 scored on search demand / fear / lead potential.
2. Packaging: 3 title + thumbnail concepts for each of the first six. Calum picks. Weak package = change the topic.
3. Look: Designer builds banner, profile image, thumbnail template (3 layouts), end-screen card. Handoff once the promise is confirmed.
4. Existing videos: new search titles, descriptions, chapters for the six long videos. Playlists: Start here / The islands / Is it safe / How to buy.
5. Lead system: one WhatsApp keyword per video, one PDF per video, PA logs source = YouTube + keyword. Check the ISLAND and FUND PDFs exist.
6. Templates: description, chapters, pinned comment, end screen.

## Phase 1 - Launch set, 1-14 Dec
- Channel page live: banner, about, links, 60-second trailer (script by Content, filmed in the first session).
- Video 1 (Islands) published - depends on the editor.
- Videos 2, 3, 4 scripted title-first, stamped by Market, pre-mortem, then filmed in ONE session so three are in the can.

Keep, change or stop, on evidence: click-through, average watched, leads.
