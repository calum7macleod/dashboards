## R4 - THE BILBAO EFFECT (needs Saadiyat + Reem in shot)
F1 "EUR84 million in. EUR500 million out in three years. This happened in Bilbao. Now it's happening in Abu Dhabi."
F2 1997. Industrial town, 25% unemployment, a dead river. A Guggenheim on it. Everyone said madness.
F3 RE-HOOK (numbers) Three years: nearly 4M visitors. Half a billion euros of activity. EUR100M in taxes - paid for itself. 82% of visitors came for the museum alone.
F4 (property) In 1996 the ground next to that museum was a derelict shipyard - worth nothing as a place to live. By 1999 flats on it sold at EUR557/sqft. Across the city, prices doubled in five years.
F5 (mechanism) Economists named it the Bilbao Effect: one landmark changes what a place is for, money follows, homes nearest it move first. Copied many times since. Rarely worked.
F6 (cut to Saadiyat) So why will it work here? Count. Louvre - open. Zayed National Museum. Guggenheim - bigger than Bilbao's. One island. Not one landmark. Three.
