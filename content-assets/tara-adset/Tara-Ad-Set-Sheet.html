AD 1 — Investor: “Join Me”

~45 seconds · numbers get on-screen text in the edit
Hooks — record all 3
  • “The world’s biggest names are moving to this island — and their people have nowhere to live.”
  • “I’ve seen every off-plan project in this market. This is the one I put my own money into — here’s why.”
  • “Demand to live on this island is exploding. Supply can’t keep up.”
Problem
  • “This island grew employment visas 50% last year.”
  • “Goldman Sachs. BlackRock. HSBC — all set up here.”
  • “Thousands of high earners arriving — and not enough quality homes for them.”
Numbers
  • “Two-beds here start from around £40,000 down — then around £30,000 every six months through the payment plan.”
  • “And my own target in net rent? Between those two numbers — every single year.”
  • “And if I was flipping? I’d be targeting at least doubling my cash invested.”
Close
  • “I’ve put my money where my mouth is — I own here myself.”
  • “Reach out if you want to join me.” DEAD STOP

AD 2 — Generic: “Wrong Famous Island”

~20–25 seconds · NO price · NO project name anywhere
Hooks — record all 3
  • “Abu Dhabi has an island problem — the wrong ones are famous.”
  • “The banks moved first. The families followed. Most investors still haven’t noticed.”
  • “There’s one island in Abu Dhabi where demand is exploding — and it’s not the one on your feed.”
Body
  • “Everyone knows Yas. Everyone knows Saadiyat. Nobody’s watching the one in between quietly filling up.”
  • “Employment visas up 50% in a year. The world’s biggest banks setting up. Homes going before people even learn the name.”
  • “By the time an island gets famous, the money’s already been made.”
  • Running long? Cut the Yas/Saadiyat line — the “money’s already been made” line ALWAYS stays.
Close
  • “Comment ‘WHERE’ — and I’ll tell you.” DEAD STOP

AD 3 — Yas Point vs Tara Park

~45 seconds · honest-odds comparison
Hooks — record all 4
  • “At Yas, 2.5 million buys you one bedroom. Ten minutes away, it nearly buys you two.”
  • “Same budget. Two islands. Very different investments.”
  • “Everyone’s buying Yas right now. Here’s what nobody’s telling you.”
  • “Do you want an investment that works for you — or one you work for?”
Problem
  • “Yas Point is everywhere right now — and the demand is real.”
  • “But it’s short-stay demand. Holiday lets. Check-ins, cleaning fees, management, admin.”
  • “That’s not a hands-off investment — that’s a part-time job. Which is great… if that’s what you want.”
Pitch
  • “At Yas Point, around 2.5 million gets you a one-bed.”
  • “For barely more, this island gives you a two-bed — bigger home, better rate per square foot.”
  • “Long-term tenants, not tourists — and demand growing because the world’s biggest names are moving in next door.”
  • “Yas gives you a busy asset. This gives you a quiet one that pays.”
Close
  • “I chose the quiet one — with my own money.”
  • “Comment ‘YAS’ or ‘TARA’ — tell me which investor you are.” DEAD STOP
SET RULES
◆ Never repeat a hook’s line inside that version’s body.
◆ “Target / targeting” on every money claim — never “guaranteed”.
◆ Every close is a hard stop. No outro, no “thanks for watching”.
◆ Ten hooks, three ads — the edit pairs each hook with the same body.
