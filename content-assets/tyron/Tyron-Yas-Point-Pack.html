Yas Point · The Setting

◆ Why Yas Island

Yas is Abu Dhabi's entertainment engine - Ferrari World, Yas Waterworld, Warner Bros., SeaWorld, the F1 circuit and Yas Mall on one island, ten minutes from the airport. That drives a deep short-stay rental market and year-round visitor demand few districts anywhere can match. Yas Point adds the missing piece: a waterfront residential quarter with its own beach, marina and retail promenade on the island's northern shore - Aldar's biggest statement here to date.
