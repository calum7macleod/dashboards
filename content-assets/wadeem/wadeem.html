06 · The mortgage route · official structure

Off-plan mortgages don't work. Until now.

Every off-plan deal in the UAE has worked the same way: you fund the developer in instalments while it's built, and at handover you either mortgage it or pay the final chunk in cash. On Wadeem Gardens, Modon and ADIB have changed that - the first off-plan villa mortgage in the country. This is the structure as Modon presented it at launch.
