06 · The mortgage route

Off-plan mortgages don't work. Until now.

Every off-plan deal in the UAE has worked the same way: you fund the developer in instalments while it's built, and at handover you either mortgage it or pay the final chunk in cash. On Hudayriyat, with ADIB, Modon has changed that - the first off-plan villa mortgage in the country.

5% down15% over 18 months80% mortgaged from there
  • Modon wants you to use it. They're actively encouraging buyers onto this structure - it's how they've priced the launch.
  • UAE residents only. If you're not a resident it can still be used to secure a better unit - I'll explain how on a call.
  • Two hours to a pre-approval. The application is simple and fast.
  • Nothing to pay the bank until two years after its first disbursement - which lands a couple of years after your own first payment. Your cash stops at 20%; the bank funds the rest.
