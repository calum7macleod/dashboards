- 2026-07-13-dashboards-crm-deck-part{1..8}of8.txt  (system build, CRM, Bashayer deck era)
- 2026-07-15-vision-journal-bashayer-part{1..6}of6.txt  (vision/3M, journal, golden visa era)

Note: secret tokens inside transcript text are auto-masked as [REDACTED] in these copies - surrounding conversation is intact. Some embedded code blocks may be garbled by the masking; all live code exists in the repo itself anyway.
