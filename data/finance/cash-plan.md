## The system: three pots, filled on payday, in this order
1. FIXED - leaves whether you like it or not. One number a month. Sits in Mashreq (AED) and BoS (GBP) before anything else moves.
2. LIVING - everything you choose. One number a week. Spent from ONE card (Wise or ADIB debit - never a credit card).
3. DEBT - whatever is left after 1 and 2. Goes to the dearest debt first. Never funds 1 or 2.
Budgeting = fill pot 1, cap pot 2, and pot 3 is what's left. Nothing else.
