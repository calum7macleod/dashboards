**One line: I show you the real numbers other agents hide - because I'm an investor who sells, not a salesman who invests.**

## THE GOAL (in order - never confuse them)
1. **Qualified inbound buyers** - DMs and calls from people ready to transact
2. **Authority** - be the name people are told to speak to about UAE off-plan
3. Followers - a byproduct, never the target

**North-star metric: SHARES** (best 28 → target 50 → 100). Secondary: saves, then DMs per video.

## THE FOUR PILLARS (every video is one of these)

**1. RECEIPTS (~40%)** - the moat. Real deals, real documents, real returns.
The Good/Bad/Ugly series · handed-over project reviews · "what this actually returned" · my own SOAs.
*Nobody else will do this. It's the whole brand.*

**2. MECHANISMS (~25%)** - the insider knowledge that makes people feel let in.
Golden Visa rules · DAMAC-mortgage-only units · payment plan maths · fees nobody mentions · how to test an agent.
*Utility = saves + shares. Ends "how do people do this?"*

**3. MARKETS & MAPS (~25%)** - the proven format. Motion, density, one thesis.
ADGM/Reem · Disney/Yas · cluster-level pricing · Dubai vs AD · UK vs Dubai.
*Your 28-share video was this. Lean in.*

**4. SKIN IN THE GAME (~10%)** - the trust multiplier.
What I bought, why, and what it does - good or bad.
*Converts. Use sparingly so it never reads as promo.*

---

## THE NON-NEGOTIABLE CRAFT RULES
1. **0-3s decides everything.** Motion in frame one, a number in the first breath, no greeting
2. **45-55 seconds.** Longer = lower completion = less reach
3. **Re-hook every 8-10s** - new number, new card, new angle
4. **One repeatable stat per video, said twice** - shares need a portable fact
5. **CTA names a receiver:** "send this to whoever's choosing between X and Y"
6. **Dead stop.** No outro, no logo, no "hope that helps"
7. **Pre-mortem before filming:** assume 1,000 views - why? Fix it first

## THE FUNNEL (content → money)
Video → DM keyword (TARA / MORTGAGE / VISA) → voice-note reply within minutes → qualify via owner's rules → two viewing slots → CRM logs next touch → deal.
**Ads amplify the winners:** any organic video that overperforms gets put behind spend with the "click here" CTA.

## MEASURE MONTHLY (not daily)
- Shares per video vs 50 target · skip rate (<35%) · saves · DMs received · leads → viewings → deals
- Log every video's 48h numbers. 5 videos in, patterns show. Kill what doesn't share; repeat what does.
