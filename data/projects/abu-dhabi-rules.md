# Abu Dhabi off-plan - the rules of the game (Calum's working conventions)
Last verified: Sep 2026 · Confidence: high on fees/conventions, medium on finance specifics

- Registration: ADM (Abu Dhabi Municipality) fee 2% of price at booking; DARI is the register/portal. No DLD - Dubai's 4% does not apply.
- Entry: 5% down + 2% ADM = ~7% day one (vs Dubai ~14%). The cheapest entry in the country - the flip-cycle argument.
- Plan shapes seen: 40/60 (Modon standard), 45/55 (Wadeem without finance), 25/75 with ADIB (bank draws 20% mid-construction + 55% at handover), 55/45 (Aldar Yas Point), 20/80 and 25/75 (Dubai South developers).
- Developer commission to the broker: 3-4% (Modon 4%), paid by the developer, usually after a paid-in threshold - board counts at signing, cash lands later.
- Resale pre-handover: buyer reimburses seller's paid equity + pays premium; ADM 2% on transfer price; developer NOC/assignment fee to confirm per developer; buyer takes over the remaining plan.
- Agency fee on resale: 2% + VAT (buyer side in Abu Dhabi practice).
- ROE convention: profit over cash deployed pre-handover (paid-in % + 2% ADM); ROI over total price. Say "indicative" and "before selling costs" every time.
- Golden Visa: Đ2M property threshold - multi-unit packs cross it.
- Register data: ADREC/DARI where available; ValuStrat, CBRE, Cavendish for market colour; DLD feed is Dubai only.
- Rent freeze: Abu Dhabi 0% since 2 Jun 2026 (indicative - primary source to confirm before camera).
