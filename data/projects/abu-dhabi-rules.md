# Abu Dhabi off-plan - the rules of the game (Calum's working conventions)
Last verified: Sep 2026 · Confidence: high on fees/conventions, medium on finance specifics

- Registration: ADM (Abu Dhabi Municipality) fee 2% of price at booking; DARI is the register/portal. No DLD - Dubai's 4% does not apply. On Reem Island (ADGM jurisdiction) the developer sheet shows "ADGM Registration Fee 2%" plus a DARI fee of Đ525 (Tara Park Phase 2 sheet, Jul 2026) - same 2%, different name; say "2% registration" and it is right either way.
- Entry: 5% down + 2% registration = ~7% day one (vs Dubai ~14%). The cheapest entry in the country - the flip-cycle argument.
- Plan shapes seen: 40/60 with 8 x 5% milestones (Modon standard: Tara Phase 2, Wadeem briefing), 45/55 (Wadeem developer graphic 22 Sep 2026 - conflicts with the 40/60, unresolved), Bashayer Phase 2 10/5/5/5/5 + 50% at handover, 25/75 with ADIB (bank draws 20% mid-construction + 55% at handover), 55/45 with 5% booking (Aldar Yas Point), 20/80 and 25/75 (Dubai South developers), 50/50 (competitor's Bashayer message, unverified).
- Developer commission to the broker: 3-4% (Modon 4%), paid by the developer, usually after a paid-in threshold - board counts at signing, cash lands later.
- Resale pre-handover: buyer reimburses seller's paid equity + pays premium; 2% registration on transfer price; developer NOC/assignment fee to confirm per developer; buyer takes over the remaining plan. Modon gate: 20% paid + NOC + admin fee (Modon SPA template - see modon.md; fee amount unknown).
- Agency fee on resale: 2% + VAT (buyer side in Abu Dhabi practice).
- ROE convention: profit over cash deployed pre-handover (paid-in % + 2% registration); ROI over total price. Say "indicative" and "before selling costs" every time.
- Service charges on file: Wadeem Gardens Đ8.32/sqft/yr on GSA, Modon provisional (Sep 2026). Blanket planning assumptions used in the Amer plan (Aug 2026): ~Đ10K/yr townhouses and villas, Đ15-20K/yr apartments - not project-specific. Nothing else per project.
- Golden Visa: Đ2M property threshold - multi-unit packs cross it. Modon issues an investor certificate (name per passport, value, paid to date, addressed to ICP/ADRO) - Tony's route, Jul 2026.
- Mortgages (from memory, verify before quoting): UAE Central Bank LTV 80% first home under Đ5M for expats, 70% above; off-plan capped at 50% - which is why the ADIB 25/75 structure matters at Đ8-12M.
- Launch mechanics (Modon): EOI holders and ADIB pre-approved buyers choose first; dates move; 2-minute unit windows. See modon.md.
- Register data: ADREC/DARI where available; ValuStrat, CBRE, Cavendish for market colour; DLD feed is Dubai only.
- Rent freeze: Abu Dhabi 0% since 2 Jun 2026, replacing the 5% cap, temporary (indicative - primary source to confirm before camera). Long-let models flat; short-let and capital-growth unaffected.

- Plan shapes as stated by Jake Nazer (reels Jul-Sep 2026, unverified): Dubai 70-80% during construction, 20-30% at handover, handover usually the only mortgageable part; Abu Dhabi - Modon 40/60 or 50/50; Aldar, Eagle Hills, Ohana 50/50; instalments linked to construction progress by law. Documented exceptions on file: Yas Point 55/45 (Aldar), Ramhan 10/40/50 (Eagle Hills), Wadeem 45/55 graphic (Modon) - his generalisation is a rule of thumb, not a sheet.
- Freehold for foreigners since 2019 - "a market seven years old" (Jake's framing; the 2019 law amendment is the fact).
- Rent cap: ADREC 5% cap became 0% on 2 Jun 2026, temporary; ADGM jurisdiction (Al Maryah, parts of Reem) is carved out per Baker McKenzie (Market h0001, 21 Sep) - check a Reem project's jurisdiction before any rent line.
