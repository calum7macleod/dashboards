## Facts (masterplan unveiling, Aug 2026)
Aldar, master developer. The final beachfront district of Saadiyat Island - "Harbor of Happiness". 744 ha / 7.4M sqm masterplan; 8 km of coastline; 18,000 residences; 58,000 future residents; 3.8M sqm GFA; 129 ha green space incl 73,800 sqm central parks; 350-berth marina (Abu Dhabi's largest, 101m superyachts); Dar al Fanun - Frank Gehry 6,000-seat House of Performing Arts; 1 km waterfront promenade; 22.5m landscape elevation for deep sea views; 2 private schools, 1 public school, 6 nurseries; NYU Abu Dhabi minutes away; 46 km cycling, 140 km walking trails; high-speed rail station planned (Dubai in 45 min - treat as upside, not baseline). Delivery 2030-2033. Representatives said "many details cannot yet be disclosed".
