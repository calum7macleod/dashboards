## Facts
Aldar. East side of Saadiyat Island, on the 6.2M sqm parcel Aldar bought in 2022 (Đ3.68bn per Cresco). Villa community marketed as "Saadiyat Lagoons - The Wilds": 4-6 bed standalone villas, GSA 4,994-6,361 sqft, mangrove setting, three zones with distinct architecture; pods optional (gym, guest suite, majlis). Launched 24 Oct 2022, sales from 3 Nov 2022: 207 villas from Đ6.1M, first phase exclusively for UAE nationals (Aldar release). Clusters named on the market: Ethir, Al Ghaf, Al Sidr, plus Cluster 5A (flat-roof minimalist, 4,960-6,420 sqft) and Cluster 2B (pitched roof, 5,020-6,350 sqft) - the Feb 2023 release of 206 villas from Đ6.4M, marketed "open for everyone". Handover: Aldar confirmed phase 1 handovers started Feb 2026 (Arabian Business, 12 Feb); Al Ghaf and Ethir from Q2 2026; Al Sidr Q3 2027 (Cresco, Aug 2026). Register rows still tag "off-plan / secondary" in Oct 2026 - transfers are contract assignments; title registration status to confirm with Aldar/DARI.
