# Hudayriyat Island (Abu Dhabi) - the district
Last verified: Sep 2026 · Confidence: medium (island stats as briefed by Modon, not independently verified)

## What it is
Modon's flagship island south-west of the city: Bashayer Residences, Hudayriyat Golf Estates, Wadeem Gardens, Al Naseem (villas, sold out, not gated), plus beach clubs, the marina, Surf Abu Dhabi, cycling track, trail park - "the lifestyle came before the houses". Villa and townhouse island first, apartments second. Modon show villa on the island (filmed Aug 2026). Source: Wadeem briefing v3 (Sep 2026), shoot scripts.

## Island facts (as briefed by Modon, Sep 2026 - "planned" = masterplan intent, not delivered)
- 52M sqm, Manhattan-sized, planned at ~2% of Manhattan's density.
- 5-minute walk to the beach from every home (Wadeem framing).
- Six tunnels off the island planned, one straight onto the Dubai road.
- Flagship mall opening next to Wadeem Gardens (briefing v3, Sep 2026).
- Land ~Đ1,500 psf at Wadeem vs ~Đ2,000 psf for a standard Dubai townhouse plot (briefing v3).
- Reem Island / ADGM workforce framed as the tenant base.
