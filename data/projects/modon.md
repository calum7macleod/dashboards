## Launch-day mechanics (Calum's own account, Bashayer Building 5 launch, Jul 2026)
- The date moves: told Thursday, expect an email the evening before saying tomorrow 10:00. Advertised 10:00 has opened at 09:00.
- Units drop in random bursts; ~2-minute window to pick a unit. Criteria agreed in advance or you lose the window.
- Confirmation is slow until chased; occasionally the SPA arrives for signing immediately.
- Priority: EOI holders choose first; ADIB pre-approved buyers get priority allocation (Wadeem, Sep 2026).
