# CALL PLAN - buyers call-through at Jake's. Built Mon 9 Nov 2026 with Mentor. PA runs it from the 07:30 brief.

## 1. Framework
The list: people who enquired with Jake over the last few months - cold, not live. Call 1 has one objective: find out where they're at.
The "because": they spoke to Jake in [month] about Abu Dhabi; Calum has joined him on that side and is picking up where they left off.

Every call ends in exactly one bucket:
| Bucket | Meaning | What happens next |
|---|---|---|
| Meeting / video call | the win - a date booked | confirm, prep two options, run it |
| Sent info | lukewarm - wants to hear when something relevant appears, not ready now | nothing time-based; tag the project/area; they hear from Calum when a matching unit or release drops |
| Webinar | yes to joining the webinar | tag only; webinar plan sits elsewhere |
| Not interested | off the list | never contacted again |
| No answer | didn't pick up | WhatsApp straight after the call; two re-calls over the following fortnight, then one message; nothing back → sent-info or drop |

Rules:
- Outcome goes on the record in the bucket's words, same day. Calum dictates, the PA logs.
- Containment: dial 09:30-11:30 and 16:00-17:00; WhatsApp replies batched 12:00 and 17:00.
- Webinar is a tag, not a stage - anyone in any bucket can carry it.
