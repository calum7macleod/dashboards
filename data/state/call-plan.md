# CALL PLAN - buyers call-through at Jake's. Built Mon 9 Nov 2026 with Mentor. PA runs it from the 07:30 brief.

## 1. Framework
Segments - one objective per call, one closing line:
| Segment | Call 1 objective | The "because" | Close |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warm book (46) | a date this week - viewing, site visit or video call | what moved since last contact: unit, price, launch | "Tuesday or Thursday?" |
| Jake's list | qualify (five facts) + permission to send two options; call 2 books | month they enquired + what changed in Abu Dhabi since | "two on WhatsApp now, I'll call tomorrow" |
| Ad lead | call inside 5 min, qualify, options same hour | the video they answered | "free Saturday to see it?" |
| Longer Term revival | one fact: has money or timing changed | a real market move since day 30 | "keep you on the list or take you off?" |

Buckets - every name in exactly one; the bucket sets the cadence; stage = bucket letter:
| Bucket | Who | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| A Live | offer or viewing booked | daily; 48h deadline on every offer |
| B Qualified | all five facts confirmed | weekly touch, content between |
| C Contacted | spoken or replied, not qualified | call day 0/1/7, WhatsApp day 0/3 |
| D New | never reached | the dialling block |
| E Longer Term | money or timing >12 months, or 3 calls + 2 messages unanswered | value message day 30/60, re-qualify day 90 |
| F Lost | criteria only: budget off the shelf, bought elsewhere, not buying in 12 months | quarterly broadcast |

Rules:
- Qualified = budget, payment route (cash/mortgage/plan), timeline <12 months, purpose (end-use/yield/capital/visa), decision-maker on the call. Four of five = C.
- Three outcomes only, on the record: next step + date / E + reason / F + criteria. Mood is never a reason.
- Two-call model on the cold list: call 1 qualifies and sends two options, call 2 books the visit. One-call-to-visit = no-shows.
- "Because" bank: five live reasons pinned above the phone, refreshed weekly (filled in section 3).
- "Who else are you speaking to" on every qualified call - compete and take them.
- Containment: dial 09:30-11:30 and 16:00-17:00; WhatsApp replies batched 12:00 and 17:00. Calum dictates outcomes in the three-outcome form; the PA logs.
- Messages: bespoke for A and B; skeleton + one bespoke line for D. Voice notes after a conversation, never as first contact.
