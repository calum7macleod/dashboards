## Max - what doesn't add up yet (for Sunday)
1. Đ850K in Q4 = Đ283K/month. Year-one months: best Đ413K (Apr), typical Đ180-250K, Sep Đ94K. At the Abu Dhabi average of Đ105K board per deal that is 8 deals in 13 weeks at a new shop, with October's own target at Đ100-200K. Off-plan pays on collection, not signing - the board can hit 850K while the bank sees a fraction before January. Runway is mid-January.
2. 2027 at Đ7M is 3x year one. Only true if ticket size moves: Wadeem-scale deals (Đ8.7-11.6M at 2-4%) are Đ200-450K each - 15-30 of those, not 67 Lagoons-sized deals.
3. 500 calls/week needs a list of thousands with numbers. The board has 53 buyers. The only list that size is the Lagoons owner data (8,843) - which is Lagoons secondary, not Abu Dhabi. Abu Dhabi has no list yet. Who are the 3,000 October calls to?
4. 20 content pieces a week + 100 calls a day + viewings = the whole day, every day. Carousels can come from Designer; reels cannot.
5. "Inbound only from Q1 2027" - three months of calling won't build an inbound engine at 4K followers. Expect the taper to be 2027 H2, not Q1.
6. Missing lines: viewings per week, WhatsApps per day, launches/events attended, deals count per period (not just coms), first Jake-era deal date, the UK Christmas roadshow, follow-up cadence for the 53 live buyers (10 already Abu Dhabi - the fastest money in the plan and it isn't in it).
7. Follower baseline unknown - content-metrics.json is not in the repo. Content to supply.
